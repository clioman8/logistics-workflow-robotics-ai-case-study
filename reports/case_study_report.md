# Case Study: Logistics Workflow Adaptation and Robotics AI Field Practice

## Abstract

This case study analyzes how a back-office and logistics field practitioner experienced warehouse workflows and how those workflows can be interpreted through robotics AI and operational intelligence.

## Case Context

Case Subject A has work experience across accounting, administration, customer service, security guidance, and logistics-center operations.

## Logistics Workflow

The logistics phase involved scanning, sorting, inspection, packing, loading support, safety awareness, and daily reporting.

## Robotics AI Interpretation

The workflow is mapped to robotics AI concepts such as AGV/AMR movement, vision-based inspection, barcode recognition, and workflow monitoring.

## Findings

1. Warehouse workflow is highly event-driven.
2. Human operators handle many exception cases.
3. Scanning and inspection are key control points.
4. Robotics AI can support repetitive movement and recognition tasks.
5. Operational intelligence requires structured event logging.

## Limitations

The case is based on resume evidence, training records, and reconstructed workflow assumptions. Further interviews are required.
